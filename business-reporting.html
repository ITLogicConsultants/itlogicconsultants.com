The business problem

Teams often combine booking, sales, or operations files manually to produce the same report each week. That can take time, create inconsistent calculations, and delay decisions. ITLOGIC can assess one defined reporting workflow and automate its repeatable steps.

Proof of concept: commercial reporting

We built a Python-based reporting demonstration for fictional transportation departures. It reads structured CSV inputs and produces reports showing seats sold, revenue, occupancy, break-even gap, booking velocity, and recommended follow-up actions based on configurable rules.

Inputs

Departure records, bookings, historical daily snapshots, and alert rules supplied as CSV files.

Automated outputs

Departure KPI CSV, route summary CSV, a browser-viewable HTML dashboard, a data-quality log, and SQLite records.

Decision support

Rule-based commercial alerts flag low booking volumes within a configured time window and suggest a review action.

Verified demonstration status: The application generated three fictional departure reports with zero reported data-quality issues in the demonstration run. Five automated unit tests passed. The browser dashboard displayed commercial intervention alerts after a test-rule configuration change. These are POC test results, not independent certification or evidence of a customer deployment.
