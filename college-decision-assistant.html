ITLogic College Decision Assistant

Explainable AI for Smarter College Decisions

The ITLogic College Decision Assistant helps students and families compare U.S. colleges using academic fit, affordability, intended major, outcomes, and personal preferences.

Instead of providing only a list of schools, the tool uses transparent scoring to help families understand why a college may fit their priorities.

Try the College Decision Assistant

Compare Colleges Using Your Priorities

Students can provide their SAT or ACT score, annual college budget, home state, intended major, and other preferences. The assistant compares available college information and organizes results into practical decision categories.

Understand the Recommendation

Results are designed to be explainable. Families can review factors such as academic comparison, affordability, major-related evidence, and outcomes rather than relying on an unexplained recommendation.

Built for Students and Families

The College Decision Assistant is intended as a decision-support tool for students and parents researching college options. It does not predict or guarantee admission.

Public pilot. College information includes data from the U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard. Decision-support guidance only; not an admission guarantee.
