What HI2P is designed to do

AI-Assisted Triage

Classify common IT issues, show confidence and review needs, and help teams prioritize incoming tickets.

Incident Correlation

Connect related reports into a shared incident so teams can investigate a wider disruption.

Role-Based Workflows

Support staff intake, technician notes and resolution, operational oversight, and executive reporting.
