This interactive demonstration shows how HI2P Pulse can analyze ServiceNow/Jira-style ticket activity and convert it into concise, manager-ready operational intelligence.

What HI2P Pulse Does

HI2P Pulse analyzes ServiceNow and Jira ticket exports to identify recurring operational patterns, cross-system signals, high-priority activity, affected areas, and week-over-week changes.

It converts ticket activity into a concise management view showing what happened, why it matters, what needs attention, and recommended next actions.

Designed for IT operations managers who need insight beyond individual tickets and standard ticket reports.
